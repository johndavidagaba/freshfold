# FreshFold privacy notice (draft)

*Written in plain language for customers in Yaba. Not reviewed by a lawyer. Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023 applies, and a lawyer should check this before launch.*

## What we collect and why

- **Your name:** so the rider knows whose laundry it is. It goes on the pickup tag.
- **Your phone number:** so the rider can call you when they arrive.
- **Your email:** to log in, to confirm the account is yours, and later to send receipts.
- **Your password:** we never store it. We store a scrambled version (Argon2id) that can't be turned back into your password.
- **When you accepted our terms:** proof that you agreed.

We don't collect your address at signup. We ask for it when you book a pickup.

## What we don't do

- We don't sell your data or share it with advertisers.
- We don't read your laundry.
- We don't track you across other websites.

## Who else sees it

- **Neon** (London) hosts our database.
- **Google** delivers our emails (Gmail).
- **Have I Been Pwned** checks whether a new password has been leaked. We send only the first 5 characters of a fingerprint of your password, never the password itself.
- **Flutterwave** will process payments when we add them. We will never see your card number.

## How long we keep it

See the [retention matrix](retention-matrix.md). In short:
- Your login stays active for 7 days.
- If you delete your account, we remove your name, email, phone and password **30 days** later.
- **Payment records are kept for years,** because the law requires it. They're kept without your name.

## Your choices

- **Delete your account** from your dashboard. You're logged out everywhere straight away.
- **Change your password** from your dashboard. Every other device is logged out.
- **Ask what we hold about you:** email us and we'll answer within 30 days. *(We don't have a support address yet: this has to exist before launch.)*

## Changes

If we change how we use your data, we'll tell you by email before the change takes effect.
