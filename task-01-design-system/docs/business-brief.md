# Business brief

## The business

**FreshFold Laundry**, a laundry and dry-cleaning service in Yaba, Lagos. It picks up clothes from homes, hostels and offices across Yaba, Surulere and Ebute Metta, washes or dry-cleans them, and delivers them back within two to four days, depending on traffic. Customers either pay per order or buy a monthly plan (a set number of items per month), which expires at the end of the month whether it is used up or not.

## The person who uses it

- **Who:** working adults and university students (UNILAG, Yabatech) in mainland Lagos, aged roughly 20 to 45. Long commutes leave little time for washing.
- **Device:** a low- to mid-range Android phone, often with a small screen.
- **Connection:** unstable 3G or 4G, small data bundles bought daily or weekly. Pages must load fast and work on a weak signal.
- **How technical:** comfortable with WhatsApp, bank apps and mobile transfers. Not comfortable with long forms or anything that looks complicated.

## The one thing that must work

**At pickup, every item collected is recorded against the customer, and the customer can see that list, the order status and what they owe.**

The most common dispute in laundry is "you collected seven items and returned six". If the item list is wrong or missing, the business cannot prove what it collected, and the customer cannot prove what they lost.

## Three questions customers ask repeatedly that only this business can answer

1. **"Are my clothes ready, and when will you deliver them?"**
2. **"How much do I owe, and did my transfer enter?"**
3. **"How many items are left on my monthly plan, and when does it expire?"**

## How the interface should feel, and why

**Calm, clean and trustworthy, and quick to scan.**

- **Clean:** the product is cleanliness. A cluttered or loud interface works against the brand.
- **Trustworthy:** customers hand over clothes and money. Numbers such as balances, item counts and dates must be impossible to misread.
- **Quick to scan:** most visits are a five-second check of status or balance on a small screen, often outdoors in bright light.

This drives the typography:

- a highly legible sans-serif with clear figures, so 1, 7 and 0 are never confused;
- strong contrast;
- large status and balance numbers;
- short labels instead of paragraphs.

No decoration that costs data.
